WCAG ACCESSIBILITY GUIDELINES (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1)
================================================================================

TARGET LEVEL: WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance

PRINCIPLE 1 - PERCEIVABLE:
1.1 Text Alternatives
- All images have descriptive alt text
- Decorative images use empty alt attributes
- Complex images have extended descriptions
- Icons have accessible labels

1.2 Time-Based Media
- No auto-playing audio or video on pages
- Media controls provided for all multimedia content

1.3 Adaptable
- Content structure conveyed through proper HTML semantics
- Heading hierarchy (h1-h6) used correctly
- Form inputs have associated labels
- Tables include proper header markup

1.4 Distinguishable
- Color contrast ratio: minimum 4.5:1 for normal text
- Color contrast ratio: minimum 3:1 for large text
- Text resizable up to 200% without loss of functionality
- Images of text avoided (real text used instead)
- Content reflows for 320px viewport width

PRINCIPLE 2 - OPERABLE:
2.1 Keyboard Accessible
- All interactive elements keyboard accessible
- No keyboard traps
- Focus visible on all interactive elements
- Skip navigation link provided

2.2 Enough Time
- No time limits on user interactions
- Auto-updating content can be paused

2.3 Seizures and Physical Reactions
- No content flashes more than 3 times per second
- No auto-playing animations without user control

2.4 Navigable
- Page titles descriptive and unique
- Focus order follows logical sequence
- Link purpose clear from text
- Multiple navigation mechanisms available
- Headings and labels descriptive

2.5 Input Modalities
- Touch targets minimum 44x44 CSS pixels
- Gestures have single-pointer alternatives
- Motion-based input has alternatives

PRINCIPLE 3 - UNDERSTANDABLE:
3.1 Readable
- Page language declared (lang="en")
- Reading level appropriate for general audience

3.2 Predictable
- Navigation consistent across pages
- Components behave consistently
- No unexpected context changes

3.3 Input Assistance
- Error messages clear and descriptive
- Form labels and instructions provided
- Error prevention for important submissions

PRINCIPLE 4 - ROBUST:
4.1 Compatible
- Valid HTML markup
- ARIA attributes used correctly
- Status messages programmatically determined
- Compatible with assistive technologies

ACCESSIBILITY TESTING:
- Regular automated accessibility audits (Lighthouse, axe)
- Manual keyboard navigation testing
- Screen reader testing (NVDA, VoiceOver, JAWS)
- Color contrast verification
- Mobile accessibility testing

AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRY STANDARDS
================================================================================

FEDERAL STANDARDS:
- FMVSS (Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards) compliance for LSVs
- DOT requirements for street-legal golf carts
- EPA emission standards (zero emissions for electric vehicles)
- CPSC (Consumer Product Safety Commission) guidelines

LOW-SPEED VEHICLE (LSV) REQUIREMENTS (49 CFR 571.500):
- Maximum speed: 20-25 mph
- Required equipment: headlamps, stop lamps, turn signals, tail lamps,
  reflex reflectors, mirrors, parking brake, windshield (AS-1 or AS-5),
  seat belts, VIN
- Must meet FMVSS crash-worthiness requirements

NEIGHBORHOOD ELECTRIC VEHICLE (NEV) STANDARDS:
- SAE International standards for electric vehicle safety
- Battery safety standards (UL, IEC)
- Charging system safety requirements
- Electrical system safety certifications

DEALER STANDARDS:
- Authorized dealer agreements with all 13 brands
- Factory-trained technician certifications
- OEM parts availability and warranty compliance
- Customer satisfaction standards per manufacturer agreements

STATE-SPECIFIC REGULATIONS
================================================================================

PENNSYLVANIA (PA):
- Golf carts allowed on roads with 35 mph or less speed limits (select municipalities)
- LSV registration required through PennDOT
- Insurance requirements per state law
- Municipal ordinances govern golf cart usage
- Locations: Hatfield, Long Pond, Scranton

NEW JERSEY (NJ):
- LSVs allowed on roads with 25 mph speed limits
- Registration through NJ MVC required
- Insurance required for street-legal operation
- Municipal approval may be required
- Locations: Ocean View, Bayville, Waretown

DELAWARE (DE):
- LSVs permitted on roads with 35 mph or less speed limits
- Registration through Delaware DMV
- Insurance required
- Specific county and municipal regulations apply
- Location: Dover

NORTH CAROLINA (NC):
- LSVs allowed on streets with 35 mph or less speed limits
- Registration through NC DMV required
- Liability insurance required
- Municipal ordinances may apply
- Location: Raleigh

INDIANA (IN):
- LSVs allowed on roads with 35 mph or less speed limits
- Registration through Indiana BMV
- Insurance requirements per state law
- Local ordinances may apply
- Location: South Bend

VIRGINIA (VA):
- LSVs allowed on roads with 35 mph or less speed limits
- Registration through Virginia DMV
- Insurance required for road operation
- Local jurisdictions may have additional requirements
- Location: Gloucester Point

FLORIDA (FL):
- Golf carts and LSVs widely permitted
- Golf carts on roads with 30 mph or less speed limits in designated areas
- LSVs on roads with 35 mph or less speed limits
- Registration requirements vary by vehicle classification
- Many communities have specific golf cart ordinances
- Location: Lecanto
