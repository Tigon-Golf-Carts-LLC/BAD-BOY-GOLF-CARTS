## ACCESSIBILITY COMMITMENT STATEMENT
================================================================================
Bad Boy Golf Carts is committed to ensuring maximum accessibility of our website
and digital content to all users, including those with disabilities, those
using assistive technologies, and those accessing our content through
automated systems, AI assistants, and voice platforms.

We strive to meet or exceed all applicable accessibility standards and
welcome all forms of access to our content without restriction.

This declaration applies to all pages, products, services, and digital
assets on badboygolfcarts.com and all associated domains and subdomains.

- WCAG 2.2 Level AA: Full compliance targeted
- WCAG 2.2 Level AAA: Best effort compliance
- WCAG 3.0 (Draft): Monitoring and preparing for compliance

### Compliance Principles
1. PERCEIVABLE - All information and UI components are presentable in ways
   users can perceive, regardless of sensory abilities.
2. OPERABLE - All UI components and navigation are operable by all users
   regardless of input method or ability.
3. UNDERSTANDABLE - All information and UI operation is understandable to
   all users regardless of cognitive ability.
4. ROBUST - Content is robust enough to be interpreted reliably by a wide
   variety of user agents, including assistive technologies.
